## Design Context

### Users

English learners who are already reading public English articles and want to turn that material into focused study without interrupting their reading flow. Their first job is to paste a URL, confirm that the right article was extracted, and continue toward a personalized lesson.

### Brand Personality

Relaxed, friendly, energetic, minimal, and trustworthy. Copy should be clear and encouraging without feeling childish, sales-driven, or overly technical.

### Aesthetic Direction

A bright, editorial-inspired light interface with warm tinted neutrals and a restrained lively accent. The landing page should feel calm and credible like a good reading tool, while the primary action feels optimistic and easy to find. Avoid generic AI gradients, dark neon styling, oversized illustrations, dashboard chrome, and card-heavy marketing layouts.

### Design Principles

1. Keep the URL-to-preview path visually dominant and remove distractions.
2. Use editorial typography and generous whitespace to signal reading quality and trust.
3. Make every loading, disabled, error, and success state explicit and reassuring.
4. Preserve full usability on mobile with comfortable touch targets and readable measure.
5. Add energy through one purposeful accent and crisp details, not decorative clutter or animation.
